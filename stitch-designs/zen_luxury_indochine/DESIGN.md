## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Emotional Tenor
This design system embodies the serene discipline of traditional tea ceremonies, incense burning, and Indochine architectural grace. Merging the tactile, artisanal rigor of Aesop with the contemplative stillness of Eastern wabi-sabi, the interface evokes a sense of paused time, mindful discovery, and organic luxury. The UI departs decisively from aggressive, hyper-commercial e-commerce patterns; instead, it establishes an environment that feels like entering a secluded tea pavilion or an atelier lined with aged agarwood and handmade Dó paper.

### Visual Language & Design Movement
The style blends **Tactile Wabi-Sabi Minimalism** with **Indochine Architectural Elegance**:
- **Organic Substrates**: Tactile surfaces reminiscent of Giấy Dó (mulberry paper) and sand-cast plaster substitute stark white screens, establishing a warm, grounded foundation.
- **Architectural Framing**: Graceful Indochine portal curves (`arch-top` motifs) define showcase imagery, balanced by rigorous typographic grid discipline.
- **Poetic Restraint**: Information breathes. Whitespace acts as visual silence, allowing the textures of agarwood, polished jade, amber resin, and cinnabar seals to command focused attention.
- **High-Touch Tactility**: Shadows remain ambient and barely perceptible, relying on fine bamboo-fiber rules, natural grain, and deliberate tonal shifts to establish hierarchy.

## Colors

### Color Philosophy
The palette draws exclusively from raw, organic matter: pressed botanicals, resinous wood cores, mineral pigments, and atmospheric smoke. Pure digital black (`#000000`) and sterile white (`#FFFFFF`) are strictly avoided across all brand and customer touchpoints.

### Key Roles & Color Hierarchy
- **Primary (`#4A3222` · Gỗ Trầm / Aged Agarwood)**: The grounding anchor of the interface. Reserved for high-priority user actions, primary call-to-action buttons, key price displays, and structural emphasis.
- **Secondary (`#C89B57` · Vàng Hổ Phách / Amber Resin)**: The touch of quiet warmth. Applied to interactive hovers, active navigational states, 5-star quality marks, and fine accents.
- **Tertiary (`#3B5E4C` · Ngọc Bích / Lotus Jade)**: Represents purity, organic origin, and natural balance. Used for botanical certifications, 100% natural badges, and serene confirmation states.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF7F2` · Giấy Dó / Rice Paper)**: The primary ambient ground, providing warmth without glare.

### Named Specialty Tokens
- `bg-warm-sand` (`#F2ECE1`): Secondary container fill used to nest cards, filter panels, and customer review modules softly against the canvas.
- `bg-agarwood-dark` (`#1E1815`): Deep, charred agarwood surface for the Zen footer, editorial moments, announcement banners, and dark administrative surfaces.
- `text-ink-black` (`#231C18`): Calligraphy-grade ink black ensuring superior legibility (contrast ratio > 12:1 against light surfaces) without clinical sharpness.
- `text-wood-muted` (`#6E5D53`): Muted bark brown for secondary descriptors, SKUs, and captions.
- `status-cinnabar` (`#A8382B`): Cinnabar mineral red for traditional chop seal accents, promotional tags, urgency cues, and critical alerts.
- `border-bamboo` (`#E5DEC9`): Subtle bamboo-fiber rule for dividers, structural frames, and outline borders.

## Typography

### Typographic Harmony & Dual System
The typography uses a thoughtful balance between poetic serif titles and clear sans-serif body text:
- **Serif Voice (`Playfair Display`)**: Conveys the deliberate rhythm of literary essays, wood carvings, and tea monographs. Used for primary headlines, category headers, and editorial quotes.
- **Sans Voice (`Be Vietnam Pro`)**: Specially crafted for Vietnamese diacritics and complex tone marks. It ensures total clarity across long product provenance descriptions, filter interfaces, checkout fields, and back-office tables.
- **Technical Monospace (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Applied selectively to batch codes, certification seals, order tokens, and SKUs (`NHANG-TRAM-40`) to create an authentic apothecary/inventory record feel.

### Rules of Usage
- Editorial headings must never be set in all-caps, maintaining a calm conversational cadence.
- Body paragraphs must respect the relaxed line-height (`1.65` to `1.7`) to give the eye generous space when reading in Vietnamese.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Philosophy
The layout is governed by a **12-column responsive fluid grid** with a hard outer container boundary of `1360px`. White space (spatial interval or *Ma* / *Khoảng Trống Thiền*) is treated as an active design element rather than an empty void. Sections breathe deeply with ample top and bottom padding (`space-xl` to double `space-xl`).

### Screen Adaptation
- **Desktop (1024px and above)**: 12-column grid, `3rem` (48px) canvas margin, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters. Catalog listings span 4 symmetrical columns. Detail views use a 2-column split (Left: Architectural Gallery; Right: Purchasing Controls).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column layout, `2rem` outer margins, `1rem` gutters. Catalog shifts into a 2-column rhythm.
- **Mobile (Below 768px)**: 4-column layout, `1.5rem` (24px) edge margins, single-column vertical flow with sticky bottom action controls.

## Elevation & Depth

### Tactile Depth Strategy
This design system rejects heavy digital drop shadows. In the wabi-sabi philosophy, objects exist resting naturally on planes of woven straw, wood, or paper. Depth is generated through **three subtle techniques**:

1. **Tonal Layering**: Containers are differentiated by surface fills: moving from the base `bg-rice-paper` (`#FAF7F2`) up to `bg-warm-sand` (`#F2ECE1`) for nested card surfaces, and plunging to `bg-agarwood-dark` (`#1E1815`) for immersive focal bands.
2. **Ghost Fiber Boundaries**: Outlines use `1px solid #E5DEC9` (`border-bamboo`), producing crisp structure that feels tactile rather than synthetic.
3. **Ambient Natural Glow**: When interactive elevation is required (such as flyout cart drawers or customer modals), the system employs an ultra-diffused, ambient shadow tinted with warm wood pigment:
   - *Drawer & Overlay Depth*: `0 16px 48px -12px rgba(35, 28, 24, 0.12)`
   - *Active Card Lift*: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(74, 50, 34, 0.08)`
4. **Substrate Texture**: A fine paper grain texture at `2%` opacity is layered over primary surfaces to soften screen glare and mimic natural craft paper.

## Shapes

### Shape Language & Architectural Motifs
The shape system combines balanced organic curvature with architectural elements:
- **Base Geometry (`roundedness: 2`)**: Standard structural elements (cards, input frames, product review containers) employ an 8px (`0.5rem`) to 12px base radius, softening technical harshness while remaining clean and structured.
- **The Indochine Arch (`arch-top`)**: The defining visual gesture of the design system. Applied to category banners, flagship collection imagery, and hero frames, the top edge forms a classic semicircular Indochine arch while the bottom remains square.
- **Botanical Pills**: Small interactive components (ingredient indicators, variant pills, stock status badges) use fully circular pill contours (`rounded-full`) to resemble smooth polished stones or tea tablets.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Thêm Vào Giỏ", "Đặt Hàng")**: Solid `bg-brand-agarwood` (`#4A3222`) fill with `text-rice-paper` (`#FAF7F2`). Borderless, subtle inner tracking (`0.02em`), 10px corner radius. On hover, the surface smoothly transitions to `bg-agarwood-dark` (`#1E1815`) with a `translate-y(-1px)` lift.
- **Secondary / Accent ("Mua Ngay")**: Subtle amber gold `border-accent-amber-gold` (`#C89B57`) with matching text on a transparent or `bg-warm-sand` ground. On hover, fills with `#C89B57` and renders text in `#1E1815`.
- **Text / Ghost ("Khám Phá Câu Chuyện")**: Ink-black text with an animated bottom border (`1px solid #C89B57`) that expands outward from center on hover.

### Product & Editorial Cards
- **Structure**: Rendered over `bg-warm-sand` (`#F2ECE1`) or transparent base with a `1px solid #E5DEC9` frame.
- **Imagery**: Encased in a `12px` rounded frame or an `arch-top` silhouette. On container hover, photography gently scales (`scale(1.04)`) over `500ms` using `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`.
- **Interactive Reveal**: The border transitions to `#C89B57`, and an "Add to Cart" action slides upward smoothly from the bottom edge.

### Chips & Variant Selectors
- **Variant Pills (Trọng lượng, Phân loại trầm)**: Outlined pill buttons in `1px solid #E5DEC9`. Active states take on a solid `bg-brand-agarwood` with crisp `#FAF7F2` text.
- **Status Badges**:
  - *100% Tự Nhiên / Organic*: Muted jade ground (`rgba(59, 94, 76, 0.1)`) with `#3B5E4C` text and a solid jade dot indicator.
  - *Ưu Đãi / Low Stock*: Soft cinnabar ground (`rgba(168, 56, 43, 0.1)`) with `#A8382B` text, reminiscent of a cinnabar seal.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- **Surfaces**: Transparent or `bg-warm-sand` with a crisp `1px solid #E5DEC9` outline.
- **Typography**: `Be Vietnam Pro` body text in `text-ink-black`. Floating labels in `text-wood-muted`.
- **Focus State**: The outline illuminates cleanly in `border-brand-agarwood` or `border-accent-amber-gold` (`#C89B57`) without any harsh blue browser rings.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Circular or soft-corner controls with `1px solid #6E5D53`. When selected, the fill transitions to `#4A3222` housing an inner amber-gold check or concentric dot.

### Specialized Brand Elements
- **Zen Dividers**: Fine horizontal divider rules (`1px solid #E5DEC9`) accented at the center with a stylized miniature lotus flower or ribbon of smoke.
- **Cart Slide-Over (Redis Drawer)**: Slides smoothly from the right viewport, backed by a soft frosted veil (`backdrop-blur-sm bg-agarwood-dark/40`), anchored in `#FAF7F2` paper substrate.
